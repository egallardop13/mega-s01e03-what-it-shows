Two causes. The ticket owns the first. The second has no ticket of its own, so its fix is queued nowhere.

The two problems

parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a date like 7 April becomes 4 July wherever the parser sees it: the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is what #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for it. Filed as ticket parser in the repo this page ships in.

hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form then reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Only the three booking-v3 tenants see it, and only through the form. Fixing the parser does not touch it; the hint or the form’s reading has to change. Filed as ticket hint in the same repo.

Both groups look identical in a report body: a month flipped. They were merged on subject line (decision 0001), so nobody had to read the body, and nobody checked which form the reporter uses.

What intake got wrong

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant on file. The browser is often not where the tenant is.

The footnote

The reports footnoted under parser in the table are the ones the desk read as a one-day move, not a month flip. Their bodies say Tuesday became Monday, or two cities got two days for one meeting. The desk’s rule files them where their tenant puts them; engineering should read those reports by hand before closing parser, because a one-day move is not what the queued fix fixes.

What to change on the desk

  • Do not merge on subject alone during a date incident. Check the tenant’s form first; the dedupe explorer already knows the answer.
  • A page that states a count should let a component count. The #4471 page typed “one issue” above a table that showed two.
